## 2. Palette Philosophy

- **Background Canvas**: Restful neutral backdrop avoiding blinding pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pure pitch black (`#000000`).
- **Surface Elevation**: Cards and nested containers lift cleanly using subtle border contrast and tone shift, NOT heavy blurry drop-shadows.
- **Single Dominant Accent**: Exactly one primary action color family. Auxiliary status colors (success, warning, danger, info) are muted and never compete with primary CTAs.
- **No Gradients on Utility UI**: Gradients are prohibited on dashboard cards, inputs, and tables. Restrict subtle gradients strictly to hero marketing accents if requested.
